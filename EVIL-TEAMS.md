Each entry below is a real output of the generator for that motive: the name, the boss, the types, and the hand-written goal, method, creed, bio and lines that every team of that motive draws on. Types and names vary between teams of the same motive; the goal, creed, method and line pools are the motive itself, so judge those.

Mark any motive to replace and I will write a new one in its slot.
